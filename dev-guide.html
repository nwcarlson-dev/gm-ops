🎯 Project Overview

GM Ops is a web-based NFL simulation game starting with a Mock Draft Simulator and expanding to a full GM career simulation experience. The goal is to build the deepest, most realistic roster-building simulation available - focused on scouting, drafting, and team construction rather than play-calling.

Product Roadmap

Phase Product Status
1 GM Ops: Draft In Development
2 GM Ops: Offseason Planned
3 GM Ops: Franchise Planned

Core Philosophy

This is a roster construction simulator, not a play-calling simulator.

  • Schemes define which skills matter - they're skill preference profiles for roster building
  • Coaches have ratings that affect simulation outcomes, not play-by-play decisions
  • Gameplan sliders allow tendency adjustments (run/pass ratio, aggressiveness, etc.)
  • The user's job is to build a roster that fits the scheme, not call plays

🏈 Branding & Identity

Brand Name

GM Ops (General Manager Operations)

Why "GM Ops"?

  • Instantly football? ✅ "GM" in sports context = General Manager building a roster
  • Modern feel? ✅ "Ops" has that clean, tech/startup energy
  • Implies the right scope? ✅ GM operations = drafting, signing, trading, cutting
  • Doesn't imply stadium management? ✅ "GM Ops" feels player/roster focused
  • Scales well? ✅ GM Ops: Draft, GM Ops: Offseason, GM Ops: Franchise
  • Easy to say? ✅ Two syllables, punchy
  • Memorable? ✅ Short, clean

  • Ball carriers (QB/RB/FB/WR/TE): Seeking contact vs. protecting self

📋 Scheme System

Schemes define skill importance weights (0-100) for each position. Weights must average to 50 across all skills for that position. Higher weight = more important for scheme fit.

Scheme Fit Calculation

Player Scheme Fit = Σ(Skill Rating × Importance Weight) / Σ(Importance Weights)

A player can be "bad" for one team and perfect for another based on skill weights.

Offensive Schemes

Scheme Philosophy Key Traits
West Coast Short, timing-based passing. YAC emphasis. High: Short Accuracy, Release Speed, Route Running, YAC
Air Raid Spread formations, 4-5 WR sets, mesh concepts. High: Release Speed, Hands, Pass Catching (RB)
Shanahan/McVay Outside Zone Wide zone running, heavy play-action, motion. High: Vision, Agility, Throw on Run, Run Blocking (WR/TE)
Power/Gap Scheme Downhill running, pulling guards, physical identity. High: Contact Balance, Tackle Breaking, Pull Ability
Spread Option / RPO Zone read, RPOs, mobile QB, numbers game. High: Mobility, Decision Making, Acceleration
Erhardt-Perkins Concept-based, multiple formations, option routes. High: Decision Making, Pocket Presence, WR Decision Making
Vertical/Deep Shot Take the top off, big plays, strong arm. High: Arm Strength, Deep Accuracy, Speed (WR)

Defensive Schemes

Scheme Philosophy Key Traits
4-3 Under Strong side overload, good vs run. High: Anchor (Nose), Pass Rush (3-tech), Run Defense (Edge)
3-4 Base Two-gap DL, versatile OLBs, disguise. High: Anchor, Double Team Occupy, Coverage (OLB)
Tampa 2 Two-deep safeties, MLB drops deep, bend don't break. High: Zone Coverage (MLB), Range (S), Pass Rush (DL)
Cover 3 / Seattle Single high safety, press corners, physical. High: Press, Contact Aggression, Range (FS)
Fangio System Multiple fronts, heavy disguise, quarters-based. High: Zone Coverage, Instincts, Versatility
Aggressive Man / Blitz Heavy Press man, heavy blitzing, take risks. High: Man Coverage, Press, Blitzing, Pass Rush

Scheme Changes

Scheme changes are rare and driven by:

  • Hiring a new coach who runs a different scheme
  • HC randomly suggests switching to better fit roster (GM can accept/decline)

The GM does NOT tell a coach to switch schemes.

Owner Relationship Meter

Goes UP: Wins, playoff runs, revenue, no controversies

Goes DOWN: Losses, scandals, empty stadium, media criticism

Trust Level What You Can Do Unilaterally
LowMinor trades, late-round picks, position coach hires
MediumMid-tier FA, coordinator hires, most trades

Owner Events

  • Owner demands you sign a specific FA (Meddler)
  • Owner vetoes a trade (Low trust)
  • Owner "suggests" a draft pick (Meddler + hometown kid)
  • Owner extends your contract after playoff run
  • Owner sells team → new owner, relationship resets

President of Football Operations

The ultimate "prestige" level for a GM career. A badge of honor for sustained success.

How to Earn It:

  • 8-10+ years as GM
  • Multiple playoff appearances
  • Division titles
  • Conference championship appearance
  • Super Bowl appearance/win accelerates timeline

What Changes:

  • Hire/Fire Authority: Can fire HC without owner approval
  • Staff Autonomy: Full control over coaching hires
  • Contract Authority: Higher spending flexibility
  • Draft Control: No owner override on picks
  • Job Security: Longer leash during rebuild
  • Legacy Features: Hall of Fame tracking, coaching tree

💡 Key Decisions Made

Decision Choice Rationale
Brand name "GM Ops" Not "The League" due to trademark conflicts
Scheme system Skill weights, not archetypes Archetypes are cosmetic only; skills determine fit
Center abbreviation "OC" not "C" Avoids confusion with cornerback
Coaching ratings All coaches have all ratings Coaches specialize but have ability across the board
FO ratings All FO have all position ratings Same principle as coaching
Scheme changes Coach-driven, not GM-driven GM hires coaches for their scheme; doesn't dictate scheme
Play calling Not simulated This is roster building, not play calling
Contact Aggression Universal skill Applies differently by position but every player has it
