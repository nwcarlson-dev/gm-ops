Enrichment Pipeline

1
Scrape Rankings
Fetches consensus rankings from NFLMDD (92 big boards aggregated) and FantasyPros (expert consensus with best/worst/avg). Updates the consensus rank for each prospect using median of all available sources.
2
Compile Scouting Reports
Scrapes scouting reports from NFLMDD player pages. Extracts strengths, weaknesses, and detailed analysis. Only processes prospects that lack detailed reports (unless overwrite is checked).
3
Generate AI Skill Grades
Uses AI to analyze scouting reports and generate position-specific skill grades (0-100) for each prospect. These skills power the scheme fit calculations during draft board generation. Processes prospects by consensus rank, prioritizing top players.
